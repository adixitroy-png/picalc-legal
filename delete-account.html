Delete in PiCalc

  1. Open PiCalc and unlock your private space with your Primary PIN.
  2. Open Stealth and scroll to Account and data.
  3. Select Delete Account. Read the confirmation and select Delete Account permanently.
  4. Stay online until PiCalc confirms deletion. It signs out and resets to the calculator. If PiCalc cannot confirm the request, retry when online or email us.

This is permanent. Mutual Burn is a separate paired-space wipe and does not delete your Supabase account. Account deletion ends the paired room while leaving the partner's local vault and chat copies in place. Your partner may need to update PiCalc before pairing again.

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion without installing or opening PiCalc. Email support@blessedpublication.com with the subject PiCalc account deletion.

Say you want your PiCalc account and associated data deleted. If available, include your PI- invite code or room identifier to help us locate your records. These identifiers locate records and do not prove ownership. We may ask for other information to verify that the account is yours. We never require your PIN, private keys, full purchase token or payment card details. If the account cannot be verified, we will explain the available next steps.

We will respond within 30 days, confirm completed deletion or explain what is still needed, and describe any applicable retention requirement.

An email request deletes server-side account data after verification. We cannot remotely erase your local vault by email. Clear PiCalc's app data or uninstall it from your devices to remove local copies you control.

What we delete and retain

  • Deleted from the service: your anonymous sign-in account, owned identities, linked referral records, join-attempt records, partner blocks involving your account, the paired room and queued relay entries associated with the account or room, and PiCalc purchase-verification receipts, including any encrypted purchase tokens, owned by your account.
  • Deleted from this device by the in-app flow: local vault photos, encrypted chats, PIN protection material, preferences, your eligibility and terms acknowledgment, and the PiCalc session. Original photos outside PiCalc remain in their original locations.
  • Surviving partner: their account, local copies and their own billing records remain. A minimal room-closure record linked to the surviving partner prevents accidental remote vault erasure. It contains no deleted account identifier or chat content.
  • Payments and required records: account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel or manage it in Google Play. Google retains its own purchase records. We may retain records required by law, such as applicable accounting records, and will explain what must be kept. Provider logs and backups may age out separately from live database records.
