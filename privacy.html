1. Summary

  • On your device: your encrypted vault, PIN-protection information, private settings and saved chat messages.
  • Through the relay: encrypted chat messages, stickers and touch signals, together with routing identifiers and timestamps. A relay message is deleted after the receiving app decrypts and successfully saves it.
  • On the service: anonymous account, pairing, referral and premium-access records can remain after a message is deleted. Undelivered messages can remain queued.
  • Never for advertising: we do not sell personal data or share it for advertising or marketing. PiCalc has no advertising or analytics SDK integration in the current build.
  • No vault uploads or card numbers: PiCalc does not upload your vault media. Google processes payments; we do not receive your card details.

2. Who we are

We are Blessed Publication, the developer of PiCalc - Stealth Calc & Vault. This policy applies to PiCalc for Android, package com.blessed.picalc, including Google Play Closed Testing and later public releases.

PiCalc looks and works like a calculator. Your private PIN unlocks its private features. Contact us at support@blessedpublication.com.

3. Information we handle

Vault photos and videos

PiCalc stores imported vault photos in encrypted app storage on your device. PiCalc does not upload vault media to Supabase, another cloud service or our servers, and we cannot access that local media remotely.

The current Android build imports photos through the system media picker. Its video entries are samples, not a working video-import feature. Local sample entries are also encrypted. This policy does not imply that video import is currently available.

Importing a photo does not delete its original from your gallery. Originals and copies you keep outside PiCalc, including any backups made by another app, are separate from the encrypted vault.

PINs and local security information

PiCalc processes your Primary and Decoy PINs on your device and does not send them to us. It does not save the PINs as readable text. It derives protection keys from the PINs using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, random salts and a device-held secret. It saves an encrypted vault key and a cryptographic Decoy PIN verifier. The device-held secret is kept through platform secure storage.

PiCalc also keeps local failed-unlock timestamps, retry/cooldown information, security settings and premium/Decoy settings. If you lose your Primary PIN or the device-held keys, we cannot recover your vault. There is no PIN recovery service.

Couple Chat and relay metadata

Chat text, stickers and touch signals are encrypted end-to-end on the sending device before they pass through Supabase to your paired partner. The private keys needed to decrypt chat content are kept on the devices, not sent to the relay. We cannot read the normal end-to-end encrypted message contents from the relay.

The relay handles message and room identifiers, sender and receiver identifiers, message type, ciphertext and creation timestamps. Pairing records contain public encryption keys, partner identifiers, a temporary SoulCode and its expiry, room creation time and premium-access status or expiry. Public keys and routing metadata are not secret message contents.

We do not maintain a permanent server-side chat-history archive. “Zero-storage relay” refers to deleting delivered relay rows; it does not mean that all server records disappear or that undelivered messages are never stored. PiCalc saves received chat messages in encrypted storage on the receiving device. Your partner can retain messages they receive.

Anonymous accounts, referrals and device settings

Network features use Supabase anonymous sign-in. This creates a server-side authentication account and identifier even though PiCalc does not ask you to create an email-and-password login. PiCalc stores an authentication session on the device and links public-key identities and invite codes to that account.

Referral processing can keep the referrer and redeemer identities, a relay-event identifier and timestamps to verify rewards and prevent duplicate credits. Pairing attempts can keep an account identifier, an attempt count and a time window for abuse prevention. These records are separate from chat ciphertext.

If you choose Share stealth invite, PiCalc opens Android’s Share Sheet with invitation text and your PI- invite code. If sharing is unavailable, PiCalc can copy that text and code to your clipboard for you to share. You choose whether and where to send it. The destination app and your device manage any copy outside PiCalc.

PiCalc stores your 18+ confirmation, acceptance of the Chat Terms and acknowledgment of the Privacy Policy on your device. It does not collect your date of birth for this confirmation.

If you block a partner, Supabase keeps the two account references, the room reference and the time the block was created. PiCalc also keeps the current room’s blocked status in encrypted local storage. Blocks prevent pairing and relay messages between the same accounts and remove queued messages between them. You can remove your own block in Stealth. Account deletion removes blocks involving that account.

Nickname, mood, anniversary, message settings and vault contents are kept in the encrypted local private data. Calculator preferences and limited non-PIN calculation history are handled locally. Motion-lock features process accelerometer readings on the device; the current code does not upload those readings.

Subscriptions and purchases

If billing is available in your build and region, Google Play handles purchases for enabled subscription and lifetime products. Google processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store card numbers or other raw payment details.

To verify a purchase and unlock shared access, PiCalc sends the product identifier, purchase token, room identifier and public-key identity to our verification function. The function checks the purchase with Google. The billing implementation stores a hash of the purchase token, account and room identifiers, product identifier, verified expiry and verification time. It updates the room's premium status. Purchase tokens are processed for verification. When scheduled billing checks are enabled, PiCalc also stores an encrypted copy of the token along with its hash and the Play order identifier, so it can recheck renewals, expiry and revocation. Restoring a verified purchase can move its entitlement to your current paired room and remove access granted by that purchase from the previous room.

Connection information and support requests

Network requests expose connection information, such as IP addresses, to the infrastructure receiving them. Supabase and Google may keep operational, security or diagnostic logs according to their service settings and policies. Log and backup retention depend on provider settings and policies. PiCalc does not promise a fixed retention period for those records. Deleting a relay row is not a promise that every infrastructure log or backup is deleted at the same moment.

If you email us, including through Report Abuse, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. Report Abuse prepares a draft that may include a room reference. You can edit it before sending. PiCalc does not attach chat messages or send the report automatically. Do not send your PIN, private keys, vault media or full purchase token in a support request.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We use Supabase for authentication, pairing, the encrypted relay and related backend records, including referral and purchase verification records where those features are enabled. Google Play processes billing and supplies purchase-verification information. See Supabase's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Your paired partner receives the messages and signals you send and the shared room's premium status. This is the communication you choose to make through PiCalc.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal process. We cannot provide locally held vault media or decrypted chat contents that we do not possess.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising or marketing. The current PiCalc build has no ads, analytics, Firebase/FCM push-notification or third-party crash-reporting SDK integration. This does not exclude the infrastructure logging described above.

6. Data security

PiCalc encrypts local private data using AES-256-GCM. Chat uses X25519 key agreement, HKDF-SHA256 key derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption. Configured Supabase connections use HTTPS and secure Realtime transport; purchase verification uses HTTPS with Google. Data sent over these service connections is encrypted in transit.

These protections serve different purposes: end-to-end encryption protects chat contents, while transport encryption protects the connection. Deleting a message is not what makes it unreadable to the relay.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device and PIN secure. Someone who can unlock your device or private space, and a partner who receives your messages, may be able to access or copy information. A calculator appearance is not itself a security guarantee.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your device

Vault items stay in PiCalc until you delete them or erase the private space. You can open a photo in Private gallery and use its Delete action. Clearing PiCalc's app data or uninstalling PiCalc removes its local vault and keys; we cannot restore them. This does not delete original media outside PiCalc or erase copies your partner has kept.

Local chat messages can remain in encrypted private storage. View-once and disappearing-message options remove messages according to the app's handling of those options; they do not guarantee that a recipient has not copied the content.

Relay and related records

After the recipient decrypts and successfully saves a message, PiCalc requests deletion of that relay row. If delivery, saving or acknowledgement fails, the encrypted row can remain for retry. The current implementation has no fixed automatic expiry for all undelivered relay rows. Deleting the room removes its queued relay rows.

Room, authentication, identity, referral and purchase-verification records are separate from messages. The current implementation does not apply a fixed automatic deletion period to all of these records. Your outgoing block remains until you unblock the partner or either account is deleted. You can request their deletion below. Any information that must be retained for a specific legal or security reason will be limited to that purpose and explained in our response.

Account deletion

Request deletion of your anonymous PiCalc account and associated server data: email support@blessedpublication.com with the subject “PiCalc account deletion”. You can make this request without opening PiCalc. Include your PI- invite code or a room identifier if available; do not include your PIN or private keys. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership and locate the correct records.

We will respond within 30 days. We will explain completion, any information needed to identify your records, and any specific retention requirement that prevents deletion of particular records. Anonymous accounts may be difficult to identify after device data has been lost.

In-app private-space deletion: unlock PiCalc, open Stealth, choose Mutual Burn · emergency wipe and confirm Burn both vaults. With a paired room, the app requests room deletion and then erases its local private data. The partner's app erases its private space when it detects the missing room after reconnecting and unlocking. If the network operation fails, the wipe may not complete; check the result.

In-app account deletion: unlock PiCalc, open Stealth, find Account and data, select Delete Account, and confirm Delete Account permanently. This removes your anonymous authentication account and associated service records, ends any paired room, and signs out and resets PiCalc on this device. Your partner keeps local copies of their photos and chats. A minimal closed-room marker for the surviving partner prevents the app from treating this as Mutual Burn.

Mutual Burn remains the separate paired-space wipe. It does not delete your anonymous account. See the PiCalc account deletion page for deletion steps, external requests and retained information.

Uninstalling PiCalc, clearing its data or deleting a room does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately in Google Play. Google's payment-record retention is governed by Google and applicable law.

8. Permissions

The app's main Android manifest declares Internet access. The packaged Android release also includes permissions contributed by its libraries, listed below. These are automatically granted normal or internal permissions, not requests for unrestricted access to your media.

Declared permissionPurposeWhen needed / choice
android.permission.INTERNETConnect to Supabase and Google Play-related services.Needed for online pairing, chat and verification. Local calculator and vault functions do not require an Internet connection.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAllow included Android components to check network connectivity.Used for network-dependent features; no runtime permission prompt.
com.android.vending.BILLINGUse Google Play purchases and restoration.Purchasing is optional; no payment is made merely by granting or declaring this permission.
com.blessed.picalc.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONSignature-level protection for internal Android component communication.Internal protection; not a user-selectable media or tracking permission.

The inspected release declares no Camera, broad photo/video-library, biometric, microphone or notification permission. Photo import uses the system picker: you choose a photo or cancel. PiCalc does not request access to your entire photo library through a broad media permission. The current voice/video samples are not microphone recording or video import.

Motion-lock uses accelerometer readings locally. You can change motion-lock settings in Stealth; it does not require a separate dangerous Android permission in the inspected release.

10. Your rights and choices

We respect the rights described below. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, including the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; receive a copy; object to or restrict certain uses; or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Available rights and exceptions differ by jurisdiction.

You can choose not to use online chat, decline photo selection, disable motion-lock, delete local items and manage subscriptions in Google Play. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully completed. We do not sell data or share it for targeted advertising.

Email us to exercise your rights or raise a privacy concern. We will respond within 30 days, verify identity only as needed, and explain any applicable limits. We can only provide or correct information we actually hold; we cannot retrieve your locally encrypted vault. You may also contact the relevant privacy authority where applicable. We will not penalize you for making a rights request.

Applicable protections and provider arrangements depend on the services and locations involved. Your local vault is not uploaded by PiCalc as part of these transfers. Contact us if you need information about the service locations and transfer arrangements relevant to your request.
