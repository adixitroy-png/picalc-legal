1. Who we are

π Calc is made by Dharmesh Jadhav, Vadodara, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the π Calc Android app.

π Calc looks and works like a calculator. Your secret PIN opens a private space with an encrypted vault and a private chat with one partner.

2. Data kept only on your phone

The following data is stored only on your phone. π Calc does not upload it.

  • Vault photos. Photos you add are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored inside π Calc. Adding a photo does not delete the original from your gallery.
  • Saved chat messages. Messages you send and receive are saved in the same encrypted storage.
  • Your PINs. Your real PIN and your decoy PIN are never stored as plain text and never sent anywhere. The app turns the PIN into an encryption key (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) together with a random secret kept in Android's secure storage.
  • Your private chat key. The secret half of your chat encryption key stays inside the encrypted vault on your phone.
  • Wrong-PIN records. Times of wrong PIN attempts and lock timers, kept in Android's secure storage.
  • Settings. Theme, shake and flip lock settings, and your 18+ and Chat Terms confirmation. We do not ask for your date of birth.
  • Calculator history. The last 30 calculations are kept in memory only while the app is open. They are not saved and not uploaded. Entries that look like a PIN are not added.
  • Sign-in token. A token for your anonymous account (see section 3), so the app can reconnect.

Android cloud backup is turned off for π Calc, so this data is not copied to your Google backup. If you uninstall π Calc or clear its storage, this data is erased and we cannot restore it. There is no PIN recovery.

Shake and flip lock reads your phone's motion sensor on the phone only. These readings are not saved or uploaded.

3. Data sent to our server

The private chat needs a server to connect you with your partner. Our server runs on Supabase (see section 5). This is everything π Calc stores there.

Anonymous account

What
A random account ID, created automatically when you first use the chat, plus the time it was created and last signed in. There is no email address, phone number, name or password.
Why
So that only your phone can collect messages sent to you and act on your room.
Deleted
When you delete your account.

Chat identity and invite code

What
The public half of your chat encryption key, and your invite code (starts with PI-).
Why
Your partner's phone needs your public key to encrypt messages for you. The invite code is used for the "invite friends" reward.
Deleted
When you delete your account.

Room (your pairing with your partner)

What
A room ID, both partners' public keys, the time the room was created, and whether the room has Pro and until when. While you are waiting for your partner, also the 6-digit SoulCode.
Why
To connect the two phones.
Deleted
The SoulCode works for 10 minutes, timed by the server. It is removed from the room when your partner joins, and replaced if you make a new one. The room is deleted when either partner uses "Clear chat and both vaults" or deletes their account.

Messages waiting for delivery

What
Encrypted messages, stickers, touch signals and "delivered" / "read" receipts. Each one also has a sender key, a receiver key, a room ID, a type (for example "text" or "receipt") and the time the server received it.
Why
So a message can wait if your partner is offline.
Deleted
As soon as your partner's phone decrypts and saves it. If it cannot be delivered, it is deleted automatically after 7 days at most. Waiting messages are also deleted when the room is deleted, when either partner blocks the other, or when either account is deleted.

Blocks

What
The two account IDs, the room ID and the time of the block.
Why
To stop messages and new pairing between the two accounts.
Deleted
When the person who blocked unblocks (Stealth → Account and data → Manage blocked partners), or when either account is deleted.

Invite rewards

What
Which invite code was used by which chat identity and when, a reward notice for the friend who invited you, and the free Pro period earned.
Why
To give each reward only once.
Deleted
When either account involved is deleted. A reward notice is also deleted when the inviting friend's app collects it or their room is deleted.

Join attempts

What
A count of SoulCode attempts in the current minute, linked to your account ID.
Why
To stop people guessing SoulCodes (at most 10 tries per minute).
Deleted
When you delete your account.

Pro purchases (only if you buy Pro)

What
The product you bought, the Google Play order ID, the expiry date, a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the Google Play purchase token, and a copy of the purchase token encrypted with a server key. We never receive your card or bank details.
Why
To check the purchase with Google, give Pro to your room, and check renewals, cancellations and refunds.
Deleted
The encrypted token copy is removed when Google Play reports that the subscription has expired or the purchase was cancelled. The rest of the purchase record is kept until you delete your account. Clearing both vaults does not delete it.

Notices for your partner

What
When you use "Clear chat and both vaults", the server keeps a short notice for your partner: the room ID, your partner's account ID and the time. When you delete your account, the server keeps an "account closed" marker for your partner: the room ID, your partner's account ID and public key, and the time. Neither contains messages, photos, keys or your account ID.
Why
So your partner's app knows what happened. A Clear-both notice tells their app to erase their vault. An "account closed" marker tells their app to end the pairing but keep their vault.
Deleted
Automatically after 365 days, or earlier if your partner deletes their own account.

Server logs

What
Our server provider keeps API and database logs, which may include your IP address and the time of each request.
Deleted
API and database logs are kept by our provider for up to 7 days. Sign-in (Auth) audit logs are kept for up to 24 hours on our current plan.

4. What the server can and cannot see

The server cannot see

  • The content of your chat messages, stickers, touch signals or receipts. These are end-to-end encrypted.
  • Your vault photos, your PINs or your private chat key. They never leave your phone.

The server can see

  • Your anonymous account ID, your public key and your invite code.
  • Who is paired with whom (as public keys and account IDs, not names).
  • For each waiting message: sender, receiver, type (for example text, sticker, touch or receipt), size, and when it arrived. Because the message is deleted when it is saved, the server can also tell roughly when it was delivered.
  • Blocks, invite rewards and Pro purchase details listed in section 3.
  • In server logs: IP address and request time, as described in section 3.

5. Other companies involved

  • Supabase hosts our server: the anonymous account, the database, live message delivery and our server functions. Supabase handles this data for us. See Supabase's privacy policy. Our provider's data centre may be outside your country.
  • Google Play handles Pro payments. When you buy, Google processes your payment under Google's privacy policy. Our server sends your purchase token to Google to check the purchase, and may check it again later for renewals, cancellations and refunds.
  • Your partner receives the messages and signals you choose to send, and sees whether the room has Pro.
  • Your email provider, if you email us. If you use "Report a problem", the app opens an email draft that includes your room reference. You can edit it, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

π Calc has no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no Firebase. We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. We cannot hand over message content, vault photos or PINs, because we do not have them.

6. Security

  • Chat encryption: X25519 key exchange, HKDF-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM. The key is made on the two phones and is never sent to the server.
  • After pairing, π Calc remembers your partner's key. If the server ever gives a different key, π Calc stops sending and shows a warning.
  • Vault encryption on the phone: AES-256-GCM.
  • All connections to our server use HTTPS or secure WebSocket (encrypted in transit).
  • Android blocks screenshots and screen recording of the private space.
  • Shake, flip or leaving the app locks the private space.

No system is perfectly safe. π Calc has not yet had an independent security audit. Anyone who knows your PIN, or your partner, can see the messages they have. Keep your PIN private.

7. How long we keep data

  • Delivered message: deleted from the server as soon as your partner's phone saves it.
  • Undelivered message: deleted after 7 days at most.
  • Invite reward notice: until the inviting friend's app collects it, or their room or either account is deleted. The 7-day limit does not apply to it.
  • SoulCode: works for 10 minutes. Removed when your partner joins or you make a new one.
  • Room: until "Clear chat and both vaults" or account deletion by either partner.
  • Clear-both notice for your partner: 365 days.
  • "Account closed" marker for your partner: 365 days.
  • Block: until unblocked or either account is deleted.
  • Anonymous account, public key, invite code, join counter, invite rewards, purchase records: until you delete your account. We do not delete inactive accounts automatically.
  • Encrypted purchase token copy: until the subscription expires or the purchase is cancelled, or until you delete your account.
  • Server logs: up to 7 days. Sign-in audit logs: up to 24 hours.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, clear both vaults, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

8. Android permissions

  • INTERNET: to connect to our server for the chat and to Google Play.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by a library the app uses, to check whether the phone is online.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: for Google Play purchases.
  • DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: an internal Android protection added automatically. It gives no access to your data.

π Calc does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification permissions, or access to all your photos. To add a photo to the vault, Android's photo picker opens and you choose one photo.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, email us (section 13).

Your account has no name, email or phone number. To find your records we need your invite code (starts with PI-) or your room reference. We may ask questions to confirm that the request is yours. We cannot give you message content or vault photos, because we do not have them.

You can also choose not to use the chat at all. The calculator and the vault work without it.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Stealth → Account and data → Delete my account. Full steps, what is deleted and what is kept are on the Delete your account page.

"Clear chat and both vaults" is different: it erases both vaults and the shared room, but keeps your account.

Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.

11. Children

π Calc is for adults 18 and over only. It is not meant for children. Before using the chat, you must confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child is using π Calc, email us and we will act on it.

See also our child safety standards.
