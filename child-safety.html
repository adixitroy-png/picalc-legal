Adults only

PiCalc is strictly for people aged 18 or older. You must confirm that you are at least 18 before using its private features. A parent or guardian who believes a child is using PiCalc may contact us.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Dharmesh Jadhav, the developer of PiCalc, prohibits all child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, solicitation, and the creation, sharing or promotion of such material. PiCalc must never be used to exploit or endanger a child.

Block and report

In PiCalc, while you are paired, open Private Chat → Room settings → Block partner and confirm Block partner. This stops messages and future pairing between the two accounts. Manage your blocks under Stealth → Account and data → Manage blocked partners. To report abuse, tap Report a problem in Room settings or under Stealth → Account and data. PiCalc opens an email draft to us with your room reference (if you have one) and space for your description. You can edit it, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself. PiCalc does not attach chats.

You can also email adixitroy@gmail.com. Describe the concern and, if available, include the room reference. Do not send your PIN, private keys or illegal images or videos. Our server cannot read end-to-end encrypted chats. When you report abuse, we see only what you choose to send us.

Our response

We review every report. When we find a violation, we can delete the accounts involved from our server. This ends their pairing and removes their server data, but it cannot remove messages or photos already saved on anyone's phone. We cooperate with law enforcement as required by law, and we will report verified violations to relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or Indian cybercrime authorities, as applicable and in accordance with law. We are committed to complying with applicable child safety laws.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child protection authority.
